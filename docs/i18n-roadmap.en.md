# i18n Roadmap

Languages: [Chinese](i18n-roadmap.md) · [English](i18n-roadmap.en.md)

P0 rule: core tutorials and promotion materials must have separate Chinese and English pages linked from the top of each page.

Priority:

1. README: `README.md` for Chinese and `README.en.md` for English.
2. Start Here: `START_HERE.zh-CN.md` and `START_HERE.en.md`.
3. Lessons: each lesson has `README.md` for Chinese and `README.en.md` for English.
4. Public release material: maintain Chinese and English as separate pages.
5. Templates: provide at least title, description, and acceptance criteria as separate English pages.

Future high-traffic languages may be AI-translated, with human review:

- Japanese
- Korean
- Spanish
- Portuguese
- French
- German
- Hindi

Do not let translation alter commands, filenames, timestamps, SRT format, or HyperFrames attributes. Do not stack both languages in one page.
