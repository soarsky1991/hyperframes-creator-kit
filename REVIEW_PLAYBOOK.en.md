# Review Playbook

Languages: [Chinese](REVIEW_PLAYBOOK.md) · [English](REVIEW_PLAYBOOK.en.md)

Every lesson or case PR must answer:

- Is the learner promise clear?
- Is there a real proof asset or clear capture plan?
- Is there a runnable or verifiable path?
- Does the material have separate Chinese and English pages?
- Is there a publishing pack?
- Did public-safety checks pass?
- Does AI B-roll avoid pretending to be real proof?
